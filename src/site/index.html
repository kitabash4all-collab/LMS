How a lesson works

Every lesson moves from idea to action to explanation.

Short, hands-on and the same rhythm every time, so children know what to expect and teachers can see who has really understood.

1Learn

Discover one clear idea

A 3-second visual hook grabs attention, then a few short cards explain the idea with everyday examples.

2Perform

Try it with your hands

Sorting, matching, budgeting, tracing letters, coding, speaking aloud. Instant feedback and hints, never just the answer.

3Probe
Why is clean water a need?
Because our body needs water every day to stay healthy. Without it we get sick, but we can live without a video game.
Teacher:Great reasoning. You compared it with a want, which shows you really understand.

Explain it in their own words

Your child writes or says why. The teacher reads it, gives feedback and adjusts the next class.

Questions

Parents often ask

What does my child need?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection. Headphones help. Everything else, including worksheets, is in the Kitabash portal.

How long are classes?

45 minutes for ages 5 to 10, and 60 minutes for ages 11 and up. A level is 8 live classes, twice a week.

What if my child misses a class?

The lesson stays open in the portal so they can catch up, and each level includes one free make-up class in another batch.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you cancel before the second live class of your first purchase, you get a full refund.

Who are the teachers?

Every teacher passes a demo class and Kitabash training, and is certified on each course before teaching it.

Are classes recorded?

Only if every child's parent in that class has switched on recording permission in their Kitabash parent account. It is off unless you turn it on. Everyone sees a “Recording” sign while it runs. Recordings are used only to check teaching quality and safety, are watched only by Kitabash staff, are never shared or used for marketing, and are deleted automatically after 30 days. You can switch permission off at any time.

Are the board courses aligned to CBSE, ICSE or IGCSE?

They are designed to support those syllabuses, and our teachers check topics against your child's school year. We do not claim official alignment.
