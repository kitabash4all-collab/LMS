Questions

Good to know

Is posting a request free?

Yes. Posting your need and the first conversation with a mentor are free. We share fees and a plan before any classes start, and you decide.

Who will contact me?

A member of the Kitabash Academy team reads every request personally and calls or messages you, usually within one working day.

Do you help with engineering and college subjects?

Yes: first-year maths, physics and chemistry, core subjects for CSE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical and Civil, programming, final-year project guidance and GATE preparation.

Will you do my assignment or project for me?

No. We teach you to do it yourself: we explain the concepts, guide your approach and review your work. That way you can explain it in your viva or exam.

Are classes online?

Mostly online (1:1 or small groups), so you can learn from anywhere. Tell us if you prefer in-person and we'll see what's possible near you.

What happens to my details?

They go only to the Kitabash Academy team and are used to contact you about this request. We don't sell or share them.
